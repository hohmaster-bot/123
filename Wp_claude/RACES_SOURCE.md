# Race texts of the owner (source for package 36, acceptance test of the core)
The owner's wording, game part only (physical descriptions and flavour left out: the catalog keeps NO general description).
Pack B (Godbound): Ankalian, Lomit, Ulstanger. Pack A (Ranniag): Azana, White maiden, Moon elf, Mordon, Reflected,
Rahim, Silver elf, Skerde, Ragmal. Units: feet only in the program. "BP" = body points, "OD" = action points.

## PACK B

### Ankalian survivor (Vyzhivshiy ankaliets)
Type: humanoid (human).
Stats: +2 Str, +2 Dex, +2 Con, +2 Cha. And +8 HP.
Run: base speed 20 ft.
Universality (common to humanity): get a feat whose prerequisites you meet.
Shadow of the crown: +2 to diplomacy and empathy. Each cycle the Ankalian gets 2 comfort if he has a reliable rear (a place where he is waited for and loved, a group of loyal friends, relatives or the like).
Adrenaline: may spend 2 BP to get +2 to all rolls for an hour. After that gets fatigue until rested. BP spent this way are restored only by a full rest.
Life among horrors: does not lower combat morale when taking critical hits and pain, and once per combat may spend 2 OD to not flee the field because of low morale.
Known languages: Kerez. Extra languages: Ancient Akeh, Stagnir.

### Lomit renegade (Lomit-renegat)
Type: humanoid (human).
Stats: +2 Con, +2 Int. And +6 HP.
Run: base speed 20 ft.
Universality: get a feat whose prerequisites you meet.
Adaptation: counts as one level higher for the purpose of exchanging comfort for training and for getting Attention.
Flexible mind: once per cycle may redistribute 2 drive points for free. Completing all existing drive quests of the cycle gives one extra Attention point.
Power of rationality: +2 to saves against any magical effects.
Known languages: Stagnir. Extra languages: Kerez.

### Ulstanger fugitive (Ulstanger-beglets)
Type: humanoid (human).
Stats: +2 Str, +4 Con. And +10 HP.
Run: base speed 20 ft.
Universality: get a feat whose prerequisites you meet.
Northerner: gets the feats Endurance and Toughness.
Rage: for each level of pain your melee weapon damage grows by 1.
Necromancy poisoning: restores half as many hit points from positive energy and gets +6 against death effects and spells of the necromancy school. Dies at 12 pain, not 8.
Known languages: Stagnir. Extra languages: none.

## PACK A

### Azana (Ranniag)
Type: humanoids (humans), mutants.
Stats: +2 Con, -2 Int.
Four-legged run: base speed 20 ft per action. With nothing in hands she may run on all fours and raises speed to 30 ft per action.
Red sun sight: darkvision 60 ft. Sees colours at half that distance. Cannot be blinded or dazzled.
Dynamo: azanas accumulate electricity and use it to attack, defend and regenerate. Get a static pool equal to 10 * level. When taking electric damage they subtract that amount from the pool, reducing damage accordingly. When they make a melee attack they subtract from the pool the number of squares moved this round and add the same amount of electric damage.
Unkillable monster: +12 HP at level 1. When hit points drop to a negative number they do not lose actions and do not get the "dying" state. Instead they get the "confused" state, except that they roll twice and choose the suitable result.
If the static pool is non-zero and hit points are below one, they get regeneration 5 (acid, fire) and lose 2 static points each turn.
If an azana deals physical damage to herself because of "confused" or for any other reason, she also turns off her regeneration for 1 round.
At the end of every session azanas reduce the number of injury points received by 4.
Hungry monster: cannot get sick or poisoned from swallowed food / water. Eats half as much food (digests inorganics partly). Drinking any unusual potion, except kinds of holy water, gives 1 extra comfort.
Known languages: two city languages.

### White maiden (Ranniag)
Type: undead (incorporeal); in a puppet body - as the puppet.
Stats: +2 Int, +2 Wis, -2 Con.
Step: base speed 20 ft per action.
Sight of the dead: ignores darkness, sees in it as in light. Senses the living within 60 ft.
Copying: gets one feat and one racial ability of the body she parasitizes (from any race template). If the body is lost she may change the racial ability.
Puppet: the base body is usually a corpse run by her power and kept by stored life force. Its loss is not critical, damage to it hardly affects her life. In negative hit points they act as usual, lower effective pain by 2 and at the start of each combat get temporary hit points equal to double the maiden's level. Natural regeneration is lowered to the minimum, healing from spells is halved.
If the body is destroyed in any way she separates and becomes a tiny haze with 5 * maiden level hit points, fly speed 10 ft (poor), unable to move except by flight, cast spells or fight normally, and gets +10 to stealth (without the size bonus). In this form she is incorporeal and usually takes no damage from ordinary physical weapons. Destroying the haze kills the maiden.
The maiden may voluntarily "drop" her body.
Possession: as a haze she can accumulate life force: spend 2d6 days unnoticed among the living draining them (causing illness), then find a recently dead corpse and pour the force into it. The body changes appearance at her taste (eyes, hair, height, physiology...).
While a haze she can attack enemies by crawling into their shadow: each round the enemy loses one action and takes d4 acid damage. Magical AoE attacks hurt the maiden as usual; positive energy deals her double damage. If the target has no shadow she cannot attack it.
While a haze she can spy on enemies: hides in the target's shadow, understands all its languages, feels with all its senses and copies all its movements. Can redirect any effect aimed at her carrier to herself if she wants. Mind control etc. is felt but does not control her if she does not want it. Each day of possession the target loses d6 Con and Cha; if the target dies this way the maiden becomes black and leaves the player's control. If the target has no shadow the possession ends.
Principledness: every white maiden has a principle (the embodiment of a character trait) because of which she has not killed people. She gets a quirk that helps her keep the principle, at the GM's discretion.
Known languages: two city languages.

### Moon elf (Ranniag)
Type: humanoid (elf).
Stats: +2 Wis, +2 Dex, -2 Con.
Vanguard: base speed 25 ft per action. The first ten feet over heavy terrain per round ignore the speed penalty.
Primal sight: darkvision 120 ft, sees colours at the whole distance. Triple vision in low light. Dazzled in bright light and gets -4 on rolls against blinding and similar.
State keeping: +6 hit points at level 1. While their hit points and BP are positive they do not lose characteristics from poisons, diseases, spells and similar effects; they may lose characteristics only from injuries.
Meditation: cannot be put to sleep. Short rest lasts 4 hours instead of 8 and they have no perception penalty during it. They may spend 3 BP to get all benefits of a long rest instead of the short one, but the BP are restored only if they are in a safe place.
Dance with blades: the first attack against them in a combat the elf may declare a miss before the roll. May add 3 spirit so that this attack makes the enemy vulnerable, or 5 spirit so that the charge of the ability is not spent.
Shadow of Santerra: twice per full rest, with three actions, walk up to four times their speed and get invisibility for d4 rounds. During it they automatically notice all incorporeal enemies within 60 ft and may attack them without breaking invisibility with a physical weapon for double damage.
Known languages: two city languages.

### Mordon (Ranniag)
Type: humanoid (human); may add the type "mutant" or any "humanoid".
Stats: +2 any characteristic, -2 Int.
Sprint: base speed 25 ft per action.
Blood cleansing: +8 hit points at level 1. If a negative status affects them they may lose 4 hit points and shorten its duration by 1 round.
Midnight instinct: do not lose the dodge bonus against melee attacks from behind. Sense an aggressive creature within 120 ft if awake.
Red sun sight: darkvision 60 ft, colours at half that distance. Cannot be blinded or dazzled.
Survival history: when taking this race template roll 1d20: 1-5 type 1, 6-10 type 2, 11-14 type 3, 15-16 type 4, 17-18 type 5, 19-20 type 6.
Type 1 Feast of Ravens: +2 Dex. Speed raised to 30 ft, immunity to fear, but must leave combat without rolls at pressure -5. The drawback can be bought out for 300 training.
Type 2 Wall of Bones: +2 Con. Free feat Toughness. No wound penalty, but -4 to CMD and doubles hit point loss from bleeding. Drawbacks can be bought out for 300 training.
Type 3 Banner of Blood: +2 Str. Specialised skill animal handling and a free mount up to CR3 at the start. Mounted gets DR 2/-. Gets 1 comfort less for fights; the drawback can be bought out for 400 training.
Type 4 Fallen of Dantalian: +2 Int. Extra weapon group, focus in the group (Flails and Chains), an extra divine spell slot of the first circle (spell chosen at the start) and mana pool +3. Always knows the arcane spells Cause Fear, Protection from Good and Ray of Enfeeblement. Their caster level for these is at least 1 even if they should not have spells. Casting characteristic is Charisma and they can cast them even without a characteristic of 11. Count as evil for all spells and supernatural abilities. Get 25% less experience, but this drawback can be bought out for 500 training.
Type 5 Goddess of Humanity: +2 Wis. While the group has a Mordon with this ability, all allies may reroll one d20 once after the result is announced; restored after the Mordon's long rest. Out of combat once per short rest the Mordon restores 10 hit points to each ally. +2 caster level for divine magic. -4 on the first five attacks in each combat, but the drawback can be bought out for 500 training.
Type 6 Return to zero: +2 Cha. Once per adventure may restore all spirit. Once per six sessions at the first death of himself or an ally he instead restores 6d6 hit points and half spirit before the deadly effect; this may cancel death. Gets no effects from the pressure of evil gods on the territory of Santerra. Loses 5 base points, but the drawback can be bought for 500 training.
Golden grandeur: may spend up to 5000 MZ on jewellery worn constantly. For each 1000 MZ spent he makes an extra roll on the survival history table (duplicates ignored) and permanently gets that history. If the jewellery is lost the bonuses of extra histories are lost; paid-off drawbacks are remembered.
Known languages: two city languages.

### Reflected (Ranniag)
Type: humanoid (human); fey.
Stats: +4 Cha, -2 Int.
Step: base speed 20 ft per action.
Rebirth: +4 hit points at level 1. Once per half-year, when they should receive deadly direct damage, they instead restore themselves to full hit points and change appearance. After the session they must randomly rearrange their characteristics and drives. They may reset their level if they want and get 10 comfort.
Red sun sight: darkvision 60 ft, colours at half that distance. Cannot be blinded or dazzled.
Self-fulfilling prophecy: between sessions the reflected may spend 1 to 20 spirit and announce a desired future. The GM gives hints, frequency depends on the spirit spent. One prophecy at a time, replaceable between sessions.
Chameleon: disguise and stealth are advanced skills and get racial +4. Diplomacy, empathy and bluff get racial +2.
Blessing of goddess Zero: +4 to all rolls during combat while the combat pressure equals zero.
Known languages: two city languages.

### Rahim (Ranniag)
Type: humanoids (humans), dragons.
Stats: +2 Str, +2 Con, -2 Int.
Step: base speed 20 ft per action.
Dragon wings: racial +4 to dexterity-feat skill (snorovka), athletics and acrobatics. For 600 training they may get fly (average) 20 ft; for 300 more upgrade flight to good.
Dragon tail: a Rahim making a melee attack may take -2 to AC until the start of his next turn and in addition to the usual attack hit with the tail. A natural attack 1d8, no extra attack penalty, iterative penalty applies, no multi-attack penalty.
Dragon sight: darkvision 60 ft, colours over the whole distance.
Dragon scales: +10 hit points at level 1. SR 12 + level and resistance to fire and electricity 5.
Intent clothed in word: because of the kotodama +4 to diplomacy and intimidation and -6 to bluff. Each time a Rahim breaks his word (in essence or letter) he loses 100 training. Only for phrases highlighted by the dragon speech; the player uses them consciously. If he has no training he sells characteristics until he can buy them back.
Known languages: Draconic, two city languages.

### Silver elf (Ranniag)
Type: humanoid (elf).
Stats: +2 Str, +2 Dex, -2 Wis.
Vanguard: base speed 25 ft per action. First ten feet of heavy terrain per round ignore the speed penalty.
Primal sight: darkvision 120 ft, colours at the whole distance. Triple vision in low light. Dazzled in bright light, -4 on rolls against blinding and similar.
Integrity: +4 hit points at level 1. While their hit points are maximal they get +2 dodge bonus to AC and racial +4 to CMB and CMD. Bleeding closes by itself in 1d4 rounds.
Pure mind: cannot be charmed, immune to the status confusion. Short rest lasts 4 hours instead of 8, no perception penalty during it. If their BP are maximal they get +2 racial to Will.
Dance with blades: same as the moon elf.
Eradication of filth: twice per full rest, with one action, declare a humanoid within 30 ft in front a heretic. The heretic makes a Will save DC 14 + elf level / 2 or for three minutes his characteristics cannot exceed 10 and he cannot use racial features or get benefits from them. Counts as a magical effect, can be reflected by spell resistance. Ends if the target is attacked by anyone except silver elves.
Known languages: two city languages.

### Skerde (Ranniag)
Type: humanoid (human).
Stats: +2 Int, -2 Str.
Run: base speed 20 ft per action.
Sea dweller: for 100 training may get swim 15 ft per action. For 100 more may hold breath for twenty minutes.
Trophy collection: skerde collect monster trophies. Destroying 2 items of any kind obtained from a particular monster (from loot) they instead get an item of category "trophy" with ilvl one higher.
For each BASE slot reserved for trophies the skerde gets +1 racial to athletics, perception and stealth.
For each two base slots reserved for trophies +1 to saves.
For each four base slots reserved for trophies +1 to Charisma.
Thorough expedition: skerde get hit points equal to the number of their base slots. This number cannot exceed 4 * skerde level.
Sense of the swell: low-light vision and blindsense 20 ft.
Flexibility of a sea predator: deal maximum damage in a grapple. If they successfully break free or slip out of a grapple they restore the actions spent on it.
Known languages: two city languages.

### Ragmal (Ranniag)
Type: humanoid (human).
Stats: +2 Con, -2 Cha.
Run: base speed 20 ft per action.
Burning gaze: eyes glow in the dark giving bright light 20 ft from the eyes. May be suppressed at will.
Magic body: +6 mana at level 1. All members of the race get this mana even without mana from a class.
All ragmals use mana to sustain life. If a ragmal has mana he must use it in priority instead of hit points when taking damage; this applies to mana from items, classes, feats and so on. May train mana at 200 training per unit of mana. Get +1 Str for each 10 maximum mana they have.
Bottomless belly: a ragmal using unusual food gets extra training equal to 10 * the ilvl of the food. For raising mana this bonus is tripled.
Blessing of the goddess of people: +3 maximum carried weight, +2 racial to Fortitude, +3 balance and doubles natural regeneration.
Heavy swing: hitting an enemy with a two-handed weapon or a natural attack the ragmal gets 2 temporary hit points until the start of his next turn.
Hunt for incarnations: at the start of combat with an instant action may spend 4 mana and get +1 fire or acid damage on all attacks or spells until the end of combat. May be activated several times and is cumulative.
Known languages: two city languages.
